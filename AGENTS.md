# Travel application engineering rules

- Build Lulu, the owner's travel social application. The Xcode project and shared scheme are named Lulu. The internal target, product and Swift module are named TravelApp. The coin catalog contains nine tiers. The owner selected `neiku_30` for the current 400-coin test tier on November 17, 2026; preserve the other eight identifiers and retain the original first-tier mapping for pending transactions.
- iOS 16+, Swift 5, UIKit, AVFoundation, MapKit, PhotosUI and StoreKit 1. iPhone only, portrait. Disable Catalyst, Mac compatibility and Apple Vision compatibility in both project.yml and generated metadata.
- Five independent navigation stacks: Shorts, Explore, Journeys, Chats, Me. Journeys is a private day-by-day itinerary, checklist, budget and journal feature connected to saved places.
- Use real, inspected travel footage and destination photographs with provenance. Rephrase travel advice in original English and link primary tourism sources. Do not portray recorded footage as live broadcasting or editorial authors as registered members.
- All project content, code, comments, documents, assets and product copy must be English. Conversational responses follow the owner's Chinese instructions.
- Present every user-facing error on every screen, including sign-in and registration, through the shared modal alert. Preserve entered values and recovery actions. Report a persistent failure once until it changes or the user retries.
- Generate each Lulu UI icon independently on transparent alpha; never extract production icons from a multi-icon sheet. Use the European travel-journal watercolor palette (deep teal, golden ochre, ivory), upright composition and clear margins. Inspect each full-size output and small-size preview for tilt, stray fragments and halos. Category filters use three horizontal slider tracks with staggered knobs; Settings uses a gear. Active provenance: Assets/generated/individual-icons-2026-11-10.json.
- App-owned icons and backgrounds must come from the image model. Use packaged, licensed custom fonts for all app-owned typography, including navigation and controls. No SF Symbols, emoji icons or code-drawn replacement icons.
- Lulu's active backend is the dedicated company project `ziryoihosvlqnbvfdvbw` in Thingtill Org. The original 139 project `dlbaneeshycwmhgfeokx` serves only the existing guest handoff in new builds. Maintain its Auth refresh and transfer endpoints until the migration window closes. Keep all business writes and purchase delivery in the company project. See docs/backend/company-cutover.md.
- Stage A uses email and password with no confirmation email. Register with matching passwords, then return to sign-in. Upgrade saved guests on the original device before retiring their credentials. Persist consent before entering the app. External Socket purchase forwarding and stage B require a later explicit continuation.
- Only the existing editorial profiles and catalog works may use owner-authorized illustrative display baselines of 10–20. Keep them separate from real interaction rows and disclose their illustrative nature in Media credits. Licensed stock portraits illustrate editorial collections and must not imply real member identities or endorsement. Two inspected Pexels CDN URLs are explicitly allowed for these portraits; other business media retains project Storage routing.
- New accounts have zero balance and empty personal collections, messages and relationships. Public editorial destination and video catalogs can be populated. Publish successful changes after authoritative persistence, preserve stable IDs and do not fabricate delivery, online status, purchase success or remote replies.
