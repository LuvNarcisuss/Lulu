# Travel application engineering rules

- Build Lulu, the owner's travel social application. The Xcode project and shared scheme are named Lulu. The internal target, product and Swift module are named TravelApp. The coin catalog contains seven owner-supplied StoreKit identifiers and two new owner-requested identifiers.
- iOS 16+, Swift 5, UIKit, AVFoundation, MapKit, PhotosUI and StoreKit. iPhone only, portrait. Disable Catalyst, Mac compatibility and Apple Vision compatibility in both project.yml and generated metadata.
- Five independent navigation stacks: Shorts, Explore, Journeys, Chats, Me. Journeys is a private day-by-day itinerary, checklist, budget and journal feature connected to saved places.
- Use real, inspected travel footage and destination photographs with provenance. Rephrase travel advice in original English and link primary tourism sources. Do not portray recorded footage as live broadcasting or editorial authors as registered members.

Use the European travel-journal watercolor palette (deep teal, golden ochre, ivory), upright composition and clear margins. Inspect each full-size output and small-size preview for tilt, stray fragments and halos. Category filters use three horizontal slider tracks with staggered knobs; Settings uses a gear. Active provenance: Assets/generated/individual-icons-2026-11-10.json.
