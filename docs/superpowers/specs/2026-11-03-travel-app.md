# Lulu

## Product

Create a native iPhone application for discovering European and North American destinations through real short videos and practical travel guides, planning personal journeys, and communicating with travelers. The owner delegated the visual direction and fifth feature and selected an independent Supabase project in the 139 organization. The owner selected Lulu as the app name. The owner supplied seven coin products and requested two additional tiers.

## Navigation and behavior

1. **Shorts:** open the tab directly into autoplay with vertically paged real footage and a search and creation toolbar, one active AVPlayer, pause/resume, progress, search and destination filters, publisher details, likes, comments/replies, saves, sharing, report/block and personal video upload with title/caption/license consent. Uploaded videos remain pending actual review. Discovery videos fill the screen behind the floating tab dock. The progress slider, interaction buttons and compact footer stay above the dock. The slider uses a one-point track and a three-point played segment with an invisible drag handle. The footer contains the title, a two-line caption and inline publisher and destination links. Pull down from the first video to refresh from the server and advance to a different available video when at least two exist. Keep the rotated order during like/save updates. The interaction rail includes the publisher avatar and opens its actual profile. Search results also support pull-to-refresh rotation and publisher avatars. Video reporting and publisher blocking appear first in More for eligible public videos. Never fabricate live broadcasting. Only the owner-authorized editorial display baselines described below may supplement actual engagement counts.
2. **Explore:** An English street map and a scrollable destination list appear in separate panes. A full-width control row contains the map/list switch and a compact filter button with three staggered slider knobs. The map fills its pane and supports pan and street-level zoom. MapLibre renders OpenFreeMap vector tiles with a bundled style that only displays explicit English map names. Region, category and All prices / Free / Paid filters appear in a popover anchored to the filter button and apply to both panes alongside destination search. Price filtering uses the featured guide shown by the cover and payment gate. Replace the toolbar refresh button with pull-to-refresh in the list and a downward pull starting within the top 52 points of the map. Successful refreshes advance the first matching destination and focus its map marker; keep the active filters and search. With fewer than two matching records, retain the available content. Failures do not report a successful refresh. Guide cards, map selections and saved guides show independently tappable publisher avatars, and member guide details show their actual author. Unassigned curated guides use the existing European or North American editorial profile, with inspected, licensed illustrative portrait photographs. Place details include photographs, practical original-English guides, source links, saved-place state, directions in Maps, share and Add to journey. Paid destination covers show a coin-price badge. The entire destination card, including its cover, title, summary and padding, opens through the same current-price and access check. Publisher avatars and names remain separate profile controls and do not trigger guide entry. This applies to destination lists, map selection cards and saved destinations. A locked paid guide requires explicit coin payment confirmation before navigation; insufficient funds offer the wallet, cancellation stays on the current screen, and free or previously unlocked guides open directly. The detail toolbar offers guide reporting and personal hiding. Hiding an editorial guide removes it from the map, destination list and saved-guide display. Settings can restore it without altering saved records or itineraries. Authors can submit their own guides with a zero or positive coin price and edit that price in My guides. Submissions remain pending until review. Paid guide bodies require a server entitlement. Cover distinctive European and North American attractions. Do not hard-code volatile opening hours or ticket prices as guarantees.
3. **Journeys:** private day-by-day itineraries, place selection/reordering/removal, trip dates, checklists, estimated budget and notes. Save changes to the authenticated owner's backend; do not invent bookings or AI responses. Empty personal journeys stay empty until created.
4. **Chats:** empty first inbox; discover actual public member profiles by name/ID; create direct or group conversations, real persistent messages, replies and read state, realtime refresh/reconnect, drafts, pinned/muted/archived state, search/filter, photo attachments, profile navigation and safety actions. Unavailable RTC calls must state their actual limitation and never show a connected call.
5. **Me:** own avatar, name, biography, truthful counts, works/liked/saved/pending collections; wallet and transaction history; edit profile; blocked/reported lists, media credits, agreements, logout and own-account deletion. The compact profile cover holds the avatar, nickname and a two-line biography, with 12-point outer artwork insets and a 64-point avatar. The information card uses a 60-percent opaque paper background so the cover artwork remains visible behind fully opaque text and the avatar. Works, Liked, Saved, Guides, Traveler Code and Pending share one category row below the wallet. Guides and Traveler Code switch the content below that row; they have no standalone buttons above it. The full traveler ID appears with the traveler code. A single gear opens Settings, which contains Edit profile. Wallet starts at zero and offers a three-by-three coin grid with StoreKit prices. Credits require verified Apple delivery and guide unlocks debit the server ledger.

## Entry and visual design

The welcome screen is the only entry screen and fits on one non-scrolling page, retaining its travel cover at the top and its title and actions near the bottom. The welcome screen includes a single agreement checkbox and two inline policy links. With the box checked, Start exploring authenticates and opens the application directly; there is no intermediate sign-in page. Starting with it unchecked opens a compact agreement dialog with the same policy links. Agree and log in accepts consent, authenticates and opens the application directly. A button activity indicator covers the request; failures keep the welcome screen and consent selection available for retry. Disagree returns to the welcome screen with the box unchecked. Restore the saved account when available or create a Supabase anonymous account for a new user. Explain access loss before a guest signs out. Persist consent version after successful write. All app-owned text uses packaged custom fonts; large editorial headings and handwritten accents evoke a travel journal. Generated raster icon family and generated watercolor/paper backgrounds use cream, deep teal and warm ochre. Real photographs and footage provide the content. The five tab roots display the Lulu compass logo at the leading edge of their top bars. The floating dock hides on pushed details, and safe areas and keyboard avoidance remain native. Accessible labels, at least 44pt tap targets, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion are required.

## Architecture and evidence

iOS 16+, Swift 5, UIKit and AVFoundation. New source is separated into shell/design, features and Supabase-backed store. The verified Teto archive is extracted outside the app as a reference; Bibi and Toti are read-only references for detail and safety behavior. Never copy their private data or integration identity. Backend stage A only: independent project, one-tap anonymous authentication, RLS, actual business data and media, chat subscriptions and versioned PDF agreements. No AI is required.

Compilation, typechecking, app launch, simulator use and runtime tests are not authorized. Produce static evidence separately from bounded backend API evidence and leave device acceptance open. Bundle ID registration, team, legal operator details, final product IDs and actual Apple purchase verification remain release dependencies.

## Public traveler profiles

Opening an author avatar shows a compact avatar/name/biography card, then a four-column Likes / Saves / Following / Followers row, followed by Follow / Message / Block actions. Report sits in the top-right toolbar. Actual Likes and Saves aggregate received engagements on published short videos without exposing other members' private collections. The editorial display totals add the separately stored owner-approved baseline. Unavailable counts display a dash and support retry. Editorial collections have no invented messaging accounts. The owner authorized fixed 10–20 illustrative display baselines on November 10; these are separate from actual member interactions. Counts for real member profiles remain actual.

Published works use one horizontal row of compact, left-aligned, tappable Shorts / Guides text headings in the custom display font. The headings have no filled background, border, rounded container or equal-width expansion. Text color indicates selection; an invisible minimum 44-point touch height preserves accessibility. Shorts is selected initially; only the selected category renders its works or empty state. The selection persists during statistics updates and return navigation. Works use a two-column grid, with one column at accessibility text sizes. Shorts use portrait 3:4 image-only tiles with edge-to-edge aspect-fill covers, no white padding, and no title or action below the image. The bottom-left overlay shows a red raster heart and the displayed like count. Tapping the image opens playback; the title remains in its accessibility label. Guide works retain white padding and place a small destination name inside the bottom-left of the image, with a red heart and like count on the next row. No title button or other action sits below either cover. Every work card is tappable. A guide card carries its own offer ID, coin price and payment confirmation; the selected guide appears first after opening. Me retains its own settings and wallet layout.
