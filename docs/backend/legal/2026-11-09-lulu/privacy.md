# Lulu — Privacy Policy
Version 2026-11-09-v2 · November 9, 2026

## Scope
This policy describes information handled by Lulu. Editorial media and linked tourism websites may come from independent sources. Opening an external website makes that website's own policies applicable. The application does not provide AI chat, live broadcasting, connected voice or video calls, or advertising personalization in this version.

## Information and purposes
A guest account uses a generated account identifier and securely stored session credentials to recognize your installation and authorize access. The service stores the profile name, biography, and avatar you choose; your likes, saves, follows, blocks, and reports; comments and video submissions; private journeys, checklists, budgets, and notes; conversation membership, messages, attachments, drafts, preferences, and read timestamps. These records support the corresponding features, prevent unauthorized access, and handle safety reports. New accounts start with empty personal records and zero credit. Payment details are not collected by this version because purchases are unavailable.

## Visibility and sharing
Destination catalogs and editorial credits are shared content. Member profile details and following relationships are discoverable by signed-in members. Comments are visible to signed-in members. Private journeys, collections, reports, pending submissions, and wallet history are restricted to their owner. Conversations and attachments are restricted to authorized participants, except profile avatars are visible to signed-in members. A recipient may independently copy or share content you send. Blocking stops subsequent messaging but does not erase earlier conversation history.

## Photos, video, and location choices
The application uploads only photos or videos you explicitly select for a profile, message, or submission. Selecting an item grants access to that selected item through the system picker. A camera or microphone permission is requested only if a corresponding feature actually needs it. Map views use destination coordinates; the application does not require continuous device-location tracking to display destinations. Directions opened in Apple Maps are handled by Apple Maps. Refusing optional media access leaves the other features available.

## Hosting, security, and retention
Supabase provides authentication, database, media storage, and realtime infrastructure. The project's primary region is in Europe; service providers may process operational and security information in other locations under their applicable terms. Network addresses and technical request information may be processed by infrastructure providers to operate and protect the service. Access controls restrict personal records and private attachments; no online service can guarantee absolute security. Profile, social, journey, and messaging records are retained while needed to provide the service or until removed through supported controls, subject to backups and legitimate security or legal retention. No fixed backup-erasure deadline is promised. See Supabase's privacy information at https://supabase.com/privacy.

## Your controls
You can edit your profile, remove your own comments and pending submissions, manage saves and follows, delete journeys, block or report members, and sign out. Settings provides an account-deletion request; deletion is complete only after successful server processing. This removes the active account and associated owned records and uploads. Copies held by recipients, external sites, protected backups, or necessary safety and legal records may remain. Signing out alone does not delete server records and may permanently prevent recovery of a guest account. Keep information you need before ending guest access.

## Updates and contact
Material changes to information handling require an updated policy and any consent required by applicable law. Lulu is being prepared for private development use. Its legal operator, and privacy-contact address remain unassigned, and no staffed contact channel is currently available. These details and any location-specific rights process must be established before public distribution.
