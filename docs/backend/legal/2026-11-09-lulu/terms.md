# Lulu — Terms of Use
Version 2026-11-09-v2 · November 9, 2026

## About the service
Lulu helps you discover editorial destination videos and guides, organize private journeys, and communicate with other travelers. Recorded footage is not a live broadcast. Editorial publishers are content sources, not necessarily registered members. Travel advice is general information. Check official sources for current access conditions, safety advice, permits, opening times, and prices before traveling.

## Your guest account
Selecting the entry action creates or restores a guest account. Keep access to this installation secure. Signing out, removing the application, losing the device, or removing its stored credentials can permanently prevent access to a guest account. Guest access is not an identity verification service. Do not impersonate another person or provide misleading profile information.

## Community and content
You are responsible for your profile, messages, comments, and uploaded media. Upload only material you own or have permission to share, including permission from identifiable people where required. Do not post unlawful, hateful, sexually exploitative, threatening, deceptive, or infringing content. Do not harass people, distribute private information, send spam, or attempt to defeat access controls. Video submissions remain private and pending review; submission does not guarantee publication or a response. The application does not promise continuous moderation. Use available reporting and blocking controls when needed.

## Social interactions
Messages are saved to the service for authorized conversation participants. A saved message is not proof that a recipient has read or received a notification. Direct conversations permit an initial greeting; mutual following is required for continued messages. Group conversations have separate membership. Blocking prevents subsequent messaging between the affected accounts. This version does not provide connected voice or video calls or emergency assistance.

## Journeys and purchases
Journeys, checklists, budgets, and journals are planning tools, not bookings or financial advice. No real reservation or travel insurance is created. Purchases and virtual credits are unavailable while the application's product catalog is unconfigured. A new account has zero credit; no payment or benefit is granted by a client-only confirmation.

## Service availability and account controls
Network failures may temporarily prevent reading or saving information. A successful save means the server accepted the change. Service features may be updated or restricted to address misuse or operational needs. Settings offers sign-out and an account-deletion request. Account deletion depends on successful server processing; a visible failure does not mean the account was deleted. Deleted content may remain temporarily in protected backups or records retained for legitimate security or legal needs. Content already copied or shared by another person cannot always be recalled.
